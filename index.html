Before you play

Nexbe is a game of chance. Every floor is an independent random draw, and the odds are built with a house edge — over time, the math favors the house, not the player.

  • Past wins or losses don't change the odds of the next tile.
  • Cashing out locks in a payout; climbing further always risks losing it all.
  • Only wager amounts you're comfortable losing entirely.
